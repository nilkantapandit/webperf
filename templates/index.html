COMMON QUESTIONS

❓ Website audit questions

Simple answers before you run a check.

What does the website speed test check?

It uses Google PageSpeed Insights to evaluate performance and several related Lighthouse categories. The report includes speed measurements, accessibility, SEO and best-practice findings.

Do I need to enter https://?

No. You can enter a domain such as example.com. The checker adds https:// automatically before testing it.

Why can my score change between tests?

Performance measurements can vary with server response time, network conditions, device emulation, page changes and third-party resources. Treat a result as a snapshot rather than a permanent score.

Does a good speed score mean the website is perfect?

No. A score is one part of a broader website health picture. Content quality, security, usability, technical SEO and business requirements also matter.

WHAT NEXT?

🔧 Fix the current site or rebuild?

A simple way to think about your next step.

Improve the existing website

Usually makes sense when the main structure is fine and the problems are things like images, caching, server settings or a few pieces of code.

  • Smaller, focused changes
  • Keep the current website and content
  • Measure the improvement as you go

Consider a rebuild

Worth discussing when the website is built on an old or difficult platform, has many deep issues, or needs a major design and technology change.

  • Modernize the foundation
  • Address structural problems together
  • Plan for easier long-term maintenance
Not sure? That's normal. A real website audit also looks at your hosting, platform, plugins, code and business needs — not just a speed score.
